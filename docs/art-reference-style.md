# Visual Art Reference & Prompt Style Guide

This document defines the standardized prompt templates and visual rules for generating AI art assets across Broadsheet & Rapier.

Art with AI can be generated in apps like: app.leonardo.ai  

---

## 1. Core Visual Anchor & Palette
All asset prompts must maintain these shared artistic rules to prevent style drift across generations:

* **Aesthetic:** 17th-century dark fantasy noir, low magic swashbuckler.
* **Art Medium:** Chiaroscuro oil painting, heavy shadows, high contrast, atmospheric, 2D game asset.
* **Color Palette:** Deep crimson, tarnished brass, dark oak, dull steel, candlelight yellows, deep umber shadows.

---

## 2. Item Icons (Weapons, Clues, Consumables)
* **Aspect Ratio:** `--ar 1:1` (Square for inventory slots and action icons)
* **Composition:** Single object, diagonal orientation, isolated on a dark parchment or leather backdrop with dramatic rim lighting.

Template:
Game item icon, single object, diagonal composition stretching from corner to corner, isolated on a dark parchment background, dramatic rim lighting on metallic and leather surfaces, 17th century dark fantasy noir, low magic swashbuckler art style, chiaroscuro oil painting, deep crimson and dark brass color palette, heavy shadows, high contrast, atmospheric, 2D game asset --ar 1:1 --description: 

---

## 3. Character Portraits (NPCs, Faction Leaders, Protagonists)
* **Aspect Ratio:** `--ar 3:4` (Vertical for dialogue windows and character cards)
* **Composition:** Waist-up or chest-up framing, facing three-quarters view, atmospheric moody background.

Template:
Game character portrait, waist-up framing, facing three-quarters view, moody atmospheric background, rim lighting highlighting face and clothing collar, 17th century dark fantasy noir, low magic swashbuckler art style, chiaroscuro oil painting, deep crimson and dark brass color palette, heavy shadows, high contrast, atmospheric, 2D game asset --ar 3:4 --description: 

---

## 4. Outdoor Locations (Districts, Alleyways, Seaports)
* **Aspect Ratio:** `--ar 16:9` (Widescreen for full-screen POI banners and environment overlays)
* **Composition:** Wide-angle perspective, wet cobblestones, flickering lantern reflections, moody fog.

Template:
Game environment landscape, wide-angle perspective, cinematic framing, wet cobblestones, flickering lantern light, moody fog and dark atmospheric depth, 17th century dark fantasy noir, low magic swashbuckler art style, chiaroscuro oil painting, deep crimson and dark brass color palette, heavy shadows, high contrast, atmospheric, 2D game asset --ar 16:9 --description: 

---

## 5. Indoor Places (Taverns, Warehouses, Print Shops)
* **Aspect Ratio:** `--ar 16:9` (Widescreen for interior inspection screens and building POIs)
* **Composition:** Cinematic wide shot, localized tallow candle or fireplace lighting, timber architecture.

Game interior environment, cinematic wide shot, detailed background elements, moody interior lighting from local candles or fireplace, timber architecture, 17th century dark fantasy noir, low magic swashbuckler art style, chiaroscuro oil painting, deep crimson and dark brass color palette, heavy shadows, high contrast, atmospheric, 2D game asset --ar 16:9 --description:
