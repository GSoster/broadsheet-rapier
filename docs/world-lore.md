# WORLD LORE BIBLE & ERA CONSTRAINTS

**Game Title:** Broadsheet & Rapier
**Engine Identity:** Thornwall
**Primary Setting:** The City of Valdeombra
**Core Era Anchor:** Equivalent to European c. 1600 (Late Renaissance / Early Baroque Swashbuckler Fantasy).

---

## 1. Technological Level (Strict Rules)
- **Firearms:** Matchlock & Flintlock pistols and muskets exist, but are slow to reload, noisy, soot-heavy, and expensive. Rapiers, daggers, sabers, and stilettoes remain the primary weapons for duels and alleyways.
- **Printing & Information:** Movable type wooden printing presses produce broadsheets, pamphlets, bounties, and scandal sheets. Public notices spread rumors and guild contracts across street corners.
- **Lighting & Energy:** Candle wax, oil lamps, gas-free lanterns, and pitch torches ONLY. No electricity, no steam power, no gas lamps.
- **Transportation:** Horses, carriages, sailing sloops, rowboats, canal barges, and walking.
- **Medicine & Science:** Barber-surgeons, alchemy, poultices, herbal medicine, early optics/telescopes.

## 2. Magic & Supernatural Rules (Low Fantasy / Noir Style)
- Magic is subtle, costly, rare, or underground (inspired by Glen Cook's *Garrett P.I.*).
- No flashy high-fantasy combat spells (no fireballs in street alleys). Alchemy, subtle street charms, curses, ancient relics, and dark secrets dominate.

## 3. Social & Cultural Landscape
- **Factions:** King's Musketeers / City Watch, Merchant Guilds, The Shadow Syndicate (Thieves' Guild), Noble Houses, Smuggler Rings, Broadsheet Guilds.
- **Public Life:** Taverns, gambling dens, fencing salons, docks, cobblestone squares, dark canals, squalid alleys, and printing houses.
- **Life in the City:** High political intrigue on top, bureaucratic equilibrium in the middle, gritty survival on the bottom.
